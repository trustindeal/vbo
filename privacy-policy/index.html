VBO (Virtual Back Office) is operated by Trustin Deal Enterprises. Our registered office is Trustin Deal Enterprises, Kasmandi Kalan, Malihabad, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226102, India.

2. Information involved

Depending on how you interact with us, this may include your name, business name, email address, phone number, enquiry text, account and login details, support messages, technical logs, device/browser information and product usage records. VBO Organiser may store the notes, schedules, goals, reminder settings and notification subscriptions you choose to create. Business solutions may process customer contact records, message data, network-status records or workforce information as configured for the relevant customer.

3. Why we use it

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, demonstrate and deliver services, operate accounts and requested features, provide technical support, prevent misuse, maintain security, handle payments or subscriptions where applicable, and meet legal obligations. We may use aggregated or appropriately de-identified information to understand and improve service performance.

4. WhatsApp, external integrations and notifications

If you contact us via WhatsApp, your interaction also involves WhatsApp/Meta's services and privacy terms. Customer-configured WhatsApp automation may process messaging contacts and content to provide the contracted service. Where you enable browser notifications, your browser's push provider may participate in delivering alerts. Integrations configured by business customers may involve external providers under their own terms.

5. Cookies, storage and technical services

The website and connected applications may use essential cookies or browser storage for navigation, preferences, sign-in and application functionality. Hosting, security, usage measurement or analytics tools, if enabled, may process limited technical information. Any non-essential tracking or marketing technologies must be separately assessed and disclosed before activation; this page is not a consent banner.

6. Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by providers necessary for hosting, infrastructure, messaging, email, payment processing or customer-requested integrations. We may disclose information where required by applicable law. We do not promise that every product uses the same providers; the precise configuration can depend on the service purchased.

7. Security and retention

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, but no website, network, device, backup, transmission or third-party platform is completely secure. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, absolute security, or that data can always be recovered. Information is retained according to the purposes for which it is processed, the relevant service configuration, legitimate operational needs, backup cycles and applicable law. Data may remain in secured backups for a limited period after removal from active systems, subject to applicable retention and deletion obligations.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to review, correct or delete personal information we control by emailing trustindeal@outlook.com. To prevent unauthorised disclosure or deletion, we may reasonably verify your identity and ask for the account or information involved. We will review and process valid requests at the earliest reasonably practicable opportunity and within any timeframe required by applicable law. Deletion may be limited or delayed where lawful retention, security, dispute resolution, technical dependencies or another legal basis applies; we will explain applicable limitations where required. VBO Organiser also includes an in-account deletion mechanism. If a business customer controls information processed through its VBO solution, we may refer the request to that customer or assist as contractually and legally appropriate. Requests are not a guarantee of immediate removal from all systems or third-party services.

9. Children

Our business and productivity solutions are not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data without appropriate permission, contact us.
