Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our standard refund rules, service cancellations, billing responsibilities and applicable legal exceptions.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. Operator and scope

VBO (Virtual Back Office) is operated by Trustin Deal Enterprises. This policy applies to services ordered directly from VBO unless a signed agreement, confirmed quotation or applicable mandatory law provides otherwise. Prices, deliverables and billing cycles are described in the relevant service order.

2. General non-refundable policy

Unless this policy, a written agreement or applicable mandatory law requires otherwise, fees paid for services, subscriptions, setup, configuration, access, licences, customisation and usage are non-refundable. A change of mind, voluntary discontinuation or non-use does not by itself create a refund entitlement. Any statutory refund, cancellation or consumer remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded remains available.

3. When VBO declines to provide a service

If Trustin Deal Enterprises itself declines to provide an accepted and paid service before delivering it, we will refund the amount attributable to the undelivered service or portion, subject to agreed treatment of any separately delivered work, incurred third-party costs that were disclosed and lawfully chargeable, and applicable law. This does not mean that an order rejected before payment creates a right to compensation. Nothing here limits additional remedies that must be provided by law.

4. Services already delivered or in progress

Where work has already been performed, access granted, resources provisioned, software configured or usage incurred, amounts attributable to that work or usage are generally not refundable, to the maximum extent permitted by law. Any contractual milestones, delivered components, accepted statements of work and legally required remedies will be considered when reviewing a request.

5. Postpaid plans and outstanding invoices

Postpaid customers are required to pay valid charges for services provided and usage incurred during the agreed billing period once the billing period has completed. Ending, pausing or not using the service does not automatically waive accrued charges. Early termination and part-cycle charges follow the relevant contract. Customers may dispute an invoice in good faith, and applicable legal or contractual credits and refunds remain unaffected.

6. Cancellation requests and timing

You may request cancellation or non-renewal through the designated support channel. The effective date depends on the plan, billing cycle, prior commitments and any agreed notice period. We may ask for identity/account verification. Any recurring payments under third-party payment arrangements may also require action through that provider. Cancellation does not automatically erase outstanding amounts lawfully due.

7. Refund review, payment method and timing

Please email trustindeal@outlook.com with the order reference, service name, payment details (excluding passwords or full card details), and reason for request. We will review the request at the earliest reasonably practicable opportunity. Approved refunds will ordinarily be made to the original payment method, where feasible, within the timeline required by applicable payment-provider rules and law. We reserve the right to verify transactions and reject requests that do not meet this policy, subject always to mandatory legal rights.

8. Pricing, taxes and third parties

Third-party services, including messaging platforms and payment providers, may have separate pricing, refunds, taxes and cancellation conditions. VBO cannot promise that independently imposed third-party charges will be refunded. Details for the relevant product must be reviewed in its quotation, billing statement or service agreement.

9. Rights reserved and non-waivable protections

Trustin Deal Enterprises reserves its lawful rights to set commercial terms, verify refunds, refuse new orders where permitted, suspend service for material non-payment subject to the applicable agreement and law, and recover valid outstanding dues. Nothing in this policy removes rights or remedies that consumers or other customers cannot waive under applicable law.

10. Governing law and jurisdiction

This policy is governed by Indian law to the extent permitted by applicable law. Subject to mandatory statutory jurisdiction and any valid service-specific agreement, courts of competent jurisdiction at Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction in relation to disputes under this policy. This does not exclude consumer forums, regulators or other authorities with non-excludable jurisdiction.
